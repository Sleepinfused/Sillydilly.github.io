# hello world ♥

hey!! welcome to my very first blog post. i'm so excited to finally have my own little corner of the internet ✦

## why i made this

i always wanted a place that felt like *mine* — not a social media feed, not an algorithm, just a place where i can write stuff, share photos, and exist online on my own terms.

neocities and the old web inspired me a lot. back then people made websites just for the joy of it. i want that energy back.

## what you'll find here

- 📝 **thoughts** — random musings, things i'm thinking about
- 📷 **photos** — stuff i've photographed that made me happy
- 🎵 **music** — what i'm listening to, songs i love
- 💭 **life stuff** — just, you know, life

## a little about me

i like long walks, good music, rainy days, and making things. this site is a work in progress and honestly that's the whole point. things don't have to be perfect to be worth sharing.
